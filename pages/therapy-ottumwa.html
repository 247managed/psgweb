Therapy & Counseling in Ottumwa, Iowa

Compassionate outpatient mental health care in Ottumwa, in person and by telehealth. Now accepting new patients.

Paula S. Gordy LISW, LLC provides outpatient mental health counseling right here in Ottumwa. Whether you are dealing with anxiety, depression, grief, trauma, a relationship strain, or a hard season of life, you do not have to travel far or wait weeks to talk to someone. We serve Ottumwa and Eddyville, Agency, and communities across Wapello County, and we also offer secure telehealth for clients who would rather meet from home.

Counseling services at our Ottumwa office

Individual Counseling

One-on-one therapy for anxiety, depression, trauma, grief, and life transitions.

Couples Counseling

Support for partners working through conflict, communication, and connection.

Group Therapy

Facilitated small groups for shared challenges and support.

BHIS for Kids

In-home and community behavioral health support for Iowa Medicaid-eligible children. Parent guide.

Insurance and Iowa Medicaid

Cost should not be the reason you put off getting help. We accept most major insurance plans and Iowa Medicaid, and we verify your benefits before your first visit so there are no surprises. See our Insurance & Billing page for details.
